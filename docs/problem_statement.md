# Problem Statement: Digital Hub Technical Knowledge & Collaboration Platform
Data Sensitivity: Internal | Date: 2025-01-09
approved_at: 2025-01-09T00:00:00Z

## Problem Statement
Technical knowledge, ongoing explorations, established solutions and relevant experience are fragmented across teams within the Digital Hub Programme Centre. As a result, teams duplicate work, miss reusable solutions, overlook concurrent initiatives, and struggle to obtain credible guidance. There is no common discovery and collaboration layer connecting problems, initiatives, solutions, knowledge and experienced contributors across the organisation.

## Business Objectives
| ID    | Objective                                      | Priority |
|-------|------------------------------------------------|----------|
| OBJ-01 | Enable teams to quickly discover relevant past work, active initiatives, established solutions and reusable assets | Must |
| OBJ-02 | Surface related or overlapping initiatives early enough for teams to reuse, align or collaborate | Must |
| OBJ-03 | Route users to relevant organisational knowledge or experienced contributors without prior knowledge of whom to approach | Must |
| OBJ-04 | Encourage teams to publish findings, proven solutions and reusable assets so that knowledge persists beyond individual projects | Should |
| OBJ-05 | Provide leadership with visibility of active exploration areas, emerging themes, overlaps, reusable capabilities and gaps | Should |

## Success Criteria
| ID    | Criterion                                                                                                | Target Metric                                           |
|-------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| SC-01 | Users can discover relevant initiatives, solutions, findings and reusable assets from a problem statement | 80% of discovery queries return at least one relevant result within the first 3 results |
| SC-02 | Meaningful overlaps between active initiatives are surfaced and relevant teams are notified                | 60% of overlapping initiatives are manually flagged by Portfolio Leads; system detects 90% automatically |
| SC-03 | Users can obtain guidance through existing knowledge or be routed to contributors with demonstrated experience | 80% of guidance requests are answered from existing knowledge assets without needing escalation to an expert |
| SC-04 | Completed and ongoing initiatives retain searchable findings, outcomes, ownership and reusable assets      | 95% of completed initiatives captured within 4 weeks of closure; 70% of ongoing initiatives share intermediate findings in-progress |
| SC-05 | Leadership can view active themes, overlapping initiatives, reusable capabilities and potential gaps       | Dashboard updated weekly showing: active theme breakdown, overlap hotspots, top 5 capability gaps, reuse rate (% of new initiatives citing prior work) |

## Primary Stakeholders (Persona-Based)
| Persona | Role | Key Interests | Touchpoints |
|---------|------|---------------|------------|
| Initiative Lead / Team Lead | Owns or sponsors a problem, exploration, or solution | Discover prior work, identify overlaps, connect with related teams, publish outcomes, ensure work is discoverable and reused | Discovery flow, initiative submission, collaboration setup, outcome logging, visibility tracking |
| Technical Contributor / Individual Contributor | Executes work within an initiative | Understand what's been tried, access proven solutions and reusable assets, find relevant expertise quickly | Discovery search, guidance routing, knowledge retrieval, solution reuse |
| Subject Matter Expert (SME) / Specialist | Deep expertise in a domain (e.g., architecture, DevOps, cloud patterns) | Be discoverable when guidance is needed, maintain expertise profile, contribute domain knowledge, stay visible as a resource | Profile/registry, guidance routing, knowledge contribution, expertise recognition |
| Enterprise Architect / Principal Engineer | Cross-cutting technical oversight and strategic guidance | See patterns across initiatives, identify overlaps and architectural consistency, assess long-term reusability, provide authoritative guidance | Portfolio view, gap identification, guidance contribution, pattern analysis |
| Content Reviewer / Moderator | Reviews contributions and updates before publication | Ensure quality, accuracy, completeness, and appropriate categorization of submissions; maintain consistent standards across the repository | Contribution review workflow, quality gates, feedback to owners, publication approval |
| Portfolio Lead / Programme Manager | Coordinates initiatives across teams and tracks portfolio health | Visibility of active work, overlapping or complementary initiatives, resource conflicts, team synergies, alignment opportunities | Portfolio dashboard, overlap detection, team notification, alignment coordination, dependency tracking |
| Senior Management / Strategy Lead | Sets direction, priorities, and investment strategy | Visibility of themes, gaps, investment ROI, organisational capability maturity, emerging strengths and weaknesses | Portfolio analytics, outcome tracking, gap analysis, capability heatmaps, investment reports |
| Platform Operator / System Owner | Maintains the discovery platform and manages data quality | Ensure system availability, data quality, correct integrations, user onboarding, knowledge taxonomies, and technical infrastructure | System health monitoring, data quality management, integration maintenance, user support, taxonomy governance |
